Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know what treatment I need to achieve a similar result?

Every smile is different. During your consultation, we evaluate your dental health, facial structure, and aesthetic goals to recommend the right plan.

Can several treatments be combined to achieve an aesthetic result?

Yes. In many cases, aesthetic results are achieved by combining procedures such as whitening, veneers, crowns, or teeth alignment.

How long does it take to see the final result?

It depends on the type of treatment. Some results can be seen on the same day, while others require several stages.

Are the results permanent?

It depends on the treatment and aftercare. Restorations such as veneers or crowns can last for many years with good maintenance.

Does smile design involve tooth wear?

Not necessarily. There are minimally invasive options depending on the case. During the evaluation we explain which technique applies to you.
