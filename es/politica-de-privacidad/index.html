Introduction

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as:

  • Contact information (name, email, phone)
  • Appointment information (date, time, service type)
  • Communication information (messages, inquiries)
  • Technical information (IP address, browser type, visit duration)

    • How We Use Your Information

    We use your information to:

  • Schedule and manage appointments
  • Communicate with you about our services
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations

    • Cookies and Similar Technologies

    We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience. You can manage your cookie preferences at any time.

  • Necessary cookies: Essential for website functionality
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how you use our site
  • Marketing cookies: To display relevant content

    • Information Sharing

    We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties, except:

  • With service providers who assist us in operating our website
  • When required by law
  • To protect our rights or safety

    • Your Rights

    You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of marketing communications

    • Security

    We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

    SMS Communications and Consent

    By providing your phone number through our forms or other communication channels, you may opt in to receive SMS messages from Novaliz Dental LLC. These messages may include:

  • Appointment reminders and confirmations
  • Customer service communications
  • Service updates and notifications
  • Marketing and promotional messages (only when explicit consent is provided)

    • SMS consent is collected separately for transactional and marketing communications. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier. You can reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance.

    Mobile Information Sharing

    No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties except aggregators and providers of the messaging services necessary to deliver SMS communications.
