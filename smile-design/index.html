Veneers and Smile Design in Miami & Hialeah

Smile design plans the shape, size and color of your teeth so they suit your face, and veneers are often how we get there. At Nova Dental Studio in Hialeah, we design smiles for patients from all over Miami, with a digital preview before treatment.

What are veneers and smile design?

A veneer is a thin shell, usually porcelain, bonded to the front of a tooth to change its color, shape or size. Smile design is the plan behind it: we look at your face, lips and teeth and decide what to change, which can mean veneers, whitening, crowns or orthodontics, alone or combined.

  1. 1Healthy gums Veneers need healthy gums around them
  2. 2Porcelain veneer A thin, custom shell made to match your smile
  3. 3Tooth to be covered Its front surface is slightly reduced first

Are veneers right for you?

Veneers suit healthy teeth whose look you want to change. They're not the answer for every smile.

Usually a good fit

  • Stained teeth that whitening can't fix
  • Chipped, worn or slightly uneven teeth
  • Small gaps between front teeth
  • Teeth that look too short or out of proportion

May need treatment or another option first

  • Large cavities or gum disease. Veneers are only an option without them
  • Teeth that are badly crooked. Orthodontics may be the better fix
  • You grind your teeth, which can damage veneers. A night guard may be needed
  • You only want whiter teeth. Whitening may be enough

We'll tell you if a simpler option, like whitening or aligners, gets you the smile you want.

How a smile makeover works, step by step

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We talk about what you'd like to change, check your teeth and gums and take photos and a 3D scan.

  2. 2

    Digital design

    We design your new smile and show you a preview, and we adjust it with you.

  3. 3

    Preparing the teeth

    We remove a thin layer of enamel so the veneers sit naturally, then take a scan. You may wear temporary veneers.

  4. 4

    Bonding

    At the second visit we check the fit and color and bond the veneers in place.

  5. 5

    Follow-up

    We check your bite and how your gums respond.

After your veneers

There's no real downtime. Most people go back to their day right after the appointment.

First days
Some sensitivity to hot and cold is normal while you get used to them.
Every day
Brush twice a day with a soft brush and a nonabrasive fluoride toothpaste, floss once a day and use an antibacterial mouthwash.
Eating
Avoid biting into very hard foods with your front teeth, and don't use your teeth to open things.
Long term
Keep regular checkups, and skip whitening products, which can scratch veneers.

Call us if a veneer feels loose or comes off. Keep it and bring it with you.

Downsides of veneers

Veneers can change a smile a lot, so it's worth knowing the trade-offs first.

  • Most veneers are permanent: the enamel removed doesn't grow back.
  • Teeth can be more sensitive to hot and cold.
  • A veneer can come loose or chip and need to be redone.
  • Their color doesn't change, so if you want whiter teeth, whiten before your veneers are made.

With good care, veneers last 10 to 15 years on average.

How much do veneers cost in Miami?

The price depends on how many teeth are involved and the material, so we give you a cost estimate after your consultation. These are the things that move it up or down:

  • How many teeth get veneers
  • Porcelain or composite
  • Whether you need whitening, orthodontics or gum treatment first
  • What your insurance covers

Veneer and smile design questions

How long do porcelain veneers last?

On average 10 to 15 years with good care. Grinding your teeth, biting hard things and skipping checkups can shorten that.

What is the downside of veneers?

Most are permanent because some enamel is removed, teeth can be more sensitive and a veneer can come loose or chip. Their color also stays the same while your other teeth may darken over time.

Can I bite an apple with veneers?

It's better to cut it into pieces. Biting into hard foods with your front teeth puts stress on veneers, so we recommend avoiding it.

Can veneers be whitened?

No. Their color doesn't change, and whitening products can scratch them. If you want a lighter shade, whiten your natural teeth first and match the veneers to them.

How many visits do veneers take?

Usually two main appointments: one to prepare the teeth and take a scan, and one to bond the veneers. The design and preview come before that.

Do veneers ruin your teeth?

They don't ruin them, but they change them for good, because some enamel is removed. The teeth underneath still need brushing, flossing and checkups.

Does insurance cover veneers?

Usually not, because most plans see them as cosmetic. If a veneer also repairs a broken tooth, part of it may be covered, so we check your plan.
