New patients

Your first visit

Your first visit begins with listening. At Nova Dental Studio, we evaluate your oral health, address your concerns and explain your options for caring for your smile. Receive personalized guidance in English or Spanish and learn about your care plan.

Call 786-687-0909 Book your appointment

New patient special

New patients without dental insurance

$89

Comprehensive exam by the dentist, clinically indicated X-rays, including a panoramic X-ray, and a regular cleaning if eligible. Deep cleaning and periodontal treatment are not included.

You will receive a personalized treatment plan recommended by the dentist. Our treatment coordinator will explain the steps, costs and available financing options to help you plan your care. Financing is subject to approval and provider terms.

Please allow approximately 90 minutes for your first visit.

Before you come

  • Bring your ID, your insurance information if you have it, a list of your medications and any relevant dental records.
  • You may complete your forms before your appointment using the link we send you, or at the office. If you prefer to complete them here, please arrive 15 minutes early.
  • If you need to cancel or reschedule, please notify us at least 48 hours in advance so we can offer that time to another patient.
  • Free parking, designated accessible parking spaces and complimentary valet parking are available.

Frequently asked questions

Who qualifies for the $89 special?

New patients without dental insurance.

Which X-rays are included?

Panoramic and individual X-rays as clinically indicated.

Is a deep cleaning included?

No; a regular cleaning only if eligible.

How long is the visit?

Approximately 90 minutes.

Can I fill out forms at the office?

Yes, please arrive 15 minutes early.

Do you offer care in Spanish?

Yes, we provide care in English and Spanish.

Are you open on Saturdays?

Our hours are Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., and Saturdays by appointment.

Can I come without an appointment?

We accept walk-ins subject to availability. For an immediate need, call before coming in.

Does my request confirm my appointment?

Not yet. The front desk will check availability and confirm your appointment.

Does my insurance cover everything?

We review your plan and its benefits. Accepting your insurance does not mean it covers every procedure.
