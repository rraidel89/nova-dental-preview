Invisalign and Braces in Miami & Hialeah

Straightening your teeth no longer has to mean a mouth full of metal. At Nova Dental Studio in Hialeah, we offer Invisalign clear aligners and braces to patients from all over Miami, and we help you choose the option that fits your teeth and your routine.

How do clear aligners and braces work?

Both move your teeth little by little with gentle, steady pressure. Clear aligners are a series of custom plastic trays, each worn for about two weeks before you switch to the next. Braces use brackets bonded to the teeth and a wire that we adjust during treatment. Either way, a retainer keeps the result once you finish.

Is orthodontic treatment right for you?

The best time is usually between ages 9 and 14, but adult treatment works just as well. It may simply take a little longer.

Usually a good fit

  • Crowded or overlapping teeth
  • Gaps between your teeth
  • An overbite, underbite or crossbite
  • Teeth that have shifted since you had braces as a kid

May need treatment or a closer look first

  • Cavities or gum disease, which need to be treated before teeth are moved
  • You know you won't wear aligners 22 hours a day. Braces may suit you better
  • Complex bite or jaw problems, which may need a specialist

At your consultation we check your teeth and bite and tell you whether aligners or braces will work better for you.

Life with aligners or braces

The first days take some getting used to. After that, it becomes part of your routine.

First days
Some soreness is normal, also after each adjustment. Acetaminophen helps. Ibuprofen and similar pain relievers may slow tooth movement, so ask us first.
Every day
Wear aligners at least 22 hours a day and take them out to eat. With braces, avoid hard, crunchy or sticky foods like nuts and caramel.
Cleaning
Brush after eating before you put aligners back in, and clean carefully around brackets.
After treatment
Wear your retainer full time for at least four to six months, then every night for the rest of your life.

Call us if a wire or bracket breaks, a wire pokes your cheek, or the pain doesn't go away.

Downsides to know about

Orthodontic treatment is safe and common, but it asks for some patience.

  • Soreness for a few days at the start and after adjustments.
  • Mouth irritation from brackets or trays while you get used to them.
  • Aligners only work if you wear them. Skipped hours mean slower progress.
  • Food trapped around brackets or under aligners raises the risk of cavities if you don't clean well.
  • Without a retainer, teeth tend to move back.

Good cleaning and wearing your retainer every night protect the result you worked for.

How much do Invisalign and braces cost in Miami?

The price depends on how much your teeth need to move, so we give you a cost estimate after your consultation. These are the things that move it up or down:

  • Clear aligners or braces
  • How complex your case is and how long it takes
  • Whether you need other treatment first, like fillings or a cleaning
  • Retainers after treatment
  • What your insurance covers

Invisalign and braces questions

Is 35 too old for Invisalign?

No. Adults can straighten their teeth at any age, and adult treatment works just as well. It may take a little longer than in teens because the jaw has stopped growing.

How many hours a day do I wear aligners?

At least 22 hours a day. You take them out to eat, drink anything other than water and clean your teeth, then put them right back in.

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

With braces, about two years on average. Some people finish in under 12 months and others need up to three years. Aligner treatment also depends on how much your teeth need to move. We give you an estimate at your consultation.

What is the biggest complaint with Invisalign?

Having to wear the aligners almost all day and take them out every time you eat. If that sounds hard to keep up, braces may be the easier choice.

Does insurance cover Invisalign or braces?

Some dental plans include orthodontic benefits, often only for children and with a lifetime limit. We check your plan and tell you what's covered before you start.

Do I need a retainer after treatment?

Yes. Everyone does. You wear it full time for at least four to six months, then at night for the rest of your life, or your teeth start to shift back.

What can I take for pain with braces?

Acetaminophen is usually the best choice for the soreness after adjustments. Ibuprofen and similar pain relievers may interfere with tooth movement, so check with us first.
