Our doctor · Founder & Lead Dentist

Liset Frias, DDS

Born in Cuba, Dr. Liset Frías came to the United States with a clear goal: to build a better future through hard work, education and discipline. She graduated from the University of Miami and has since dedicated her career to modern, precise, patient-centered dentistry.

Her motivation goes beyond fixing teeth. She wants each patient to regain confidence, improve their quality of life and smile again with peace of mind. That's why she combines clinical experience with the latest dental technology and designs plans tailored to each case, caring for both health and appearance.

Her approach includes prevention, restorative treatments and advanced solutions such as smile design, implants and cosmetic dentistry, always with clear, professional care.

Our story

Nova Dental Studio was founded on the belief that dentistry should be personal, compassionate and empowering. What began as Dr. Liset Frías' dream after arriving from Cuba has grown into a dedicated team of professionals helping Miami families smile with confidence.

Together, we combine advanced technology with genuine care, treating each patient like family and making every visit comfortable, transparent and worthwhile. At Nova, your smile is our passion and your trust is our greatest achievement.

Our approach

Our care method

A structured and transparent approach that prioritizes your comfort, safety, and peace of mind.

  1. Book your appointment

    Schedule your visit easily online or by phone and choose the time that suits you best.

  2. Initial consultation

    Meet the doctor, discuss your concerns and goals, and receive a personalized evaluation.

  3. Complete dental exam

    If necessary, we perform digital exams, X-rays, and a complete evaluation to understand your oral health.

  4. Treatment plan and next steps

    Receive your personalized treatment plan and financing options, and schedule your procedure.
